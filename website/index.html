How it works

Presence is the whole product.

Anyone can post anything from anywhere. BlockTalk can't — and that's the point. What you read is coming from people who are actually there.

📍

Post where you are

You can only post in the neighborhood you're physically in. That constraint is what makes the feed real instead of performative.

👀

Read anywhere

Browse any block in the city from your couch. Reading and voting work from anywhere — posting is the only thing that's presence-locked.

🗺️

Drop it on the map

Tag the exact corner or business where something happened. Your comment lives on the spot, for the next person who walks by.

One rule
Anonymity isn't a hall pass.

You're anonymous to your neighbors — not to us, and not to the law. Hate, harassment, and identifying private people get you banned. Everything else — the honest takes, the neighborhood beef, the jokes — is fair game.
