Support

We're here to help

1Contact us

For any questions, issues, or feedback, email us at hello@blocktalk.nyc. We typically respond within 24 hours.

2Frequently asked questions

How does posting work?

You can only post in the neighborhood you're physically located in. BlockTalk uses your location to verify presence. Reading, replying, and voting work from anywhere in the city.

Why can't I post?

Make sure location services are enabled for BlockTalk in your device settings. You need to be physically within a NYC neighborhood to post there. If you're between neighborhoods or in an area we haven't mapped yet, try moving to a more central spot.

How do I change my username?

Go to Settings in the app. You can change your username once every 30 days.

How do I change my home neighborhood?

Go to Settings in the app. You can change your home neighborhood once every 90 days.

How do I report a post?

Tap the three-dot menu on any post and select "Report." Choose a reason and we'll review it. Posts that receive multiple reports are automatically hidden pending review.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, posts, replies, and votes. You can also email hello@blocktalk.nyc to request deletion.

Is BlockTalk anonymous?

Your real name is never shown to other users — they only see your username. However, you are not anonymous to us or to the law. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug or have a feature request

Email hello@blocktalk.nyc with as much detail as possible. Screenshots help.
